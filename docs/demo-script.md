# Five-minute demo script

## 0:00 — The problem

Show the raw CAP text in **Alert clarity lab**. Read two lines. Say: “This is not another chatbot. It is a proof-carrying protocol that turns one authoritative disaster record into web, SMS, voice, and offline instructions without letting AI change critical facts. The last-mile failure is whether a stressed household can act—and verify why.”

## 0:35 — A citizen starts with a need

Open **Find help** with `24370`. The protocol detects that the ZIP crosses the Smyth/Washington county boundary and asks the one location question that can change the declaration result. Choose **Smyth County**. Select Home repairs, Temporary housing, and Lost documents; mark “I cannot stay at home” and “I lost my identification.” Generate the plan.

Point out:

- the clearly labeled historical Helene replay and passed deadline;
- the “17 possible / 3 asked / 14 skipped” information-gain audit;
- the same locked facts compiled into Web, SMS, Voice, and Offline tabs;
- the anonymous `CONTINUE RBX-…` recovery command;
- confidence labels and official source links;
- document alternatives without uploading documents;
- the privacy receipt;
- the 211 handoff summary.

## 1:50 — Better than a phone tree

Open the first three result cards. Say: “The packet is the source of truth. Foundry explains reviewed records; deterministic rules choose the candidates and compile each channel; the agency decides eligibility.”

## 2:25 — The refusal beat

Open **Alert clarity lab**. Point to the Before/During and After lifecycle band. Run the Spanish transform, switch across its Web/SMS/Voice/Offline payloads, and play audio. Turn on **Test refusal** and run again.

Pause on the orange segment. Say: “A critical entity was removed. The system withheld that segment, showed exact source English, and asked for an interpreter. It did not fail the whole alert, and it did not guess.”

## 3:15 — Verify the chain

Click **Verify this result**. Show the signature and content match. Say unprompted:

“NWS CAP alerts are not individually end-to-end signed. For a live alert, our production claim is traceability to a payload fetched over TLS plus our signature over the transformation—not an NWS signature. This screen is a synthetic fixture and says so.”

## 3:50 — Fraud and resilience

Open **Fraud check**, load the suspicious example, and run it. Then mention the PWA/print path, device speech fallback, cached alert, and that real IPAWS requires a COG agreement.

## 4:25 — Rubric close

- **Performance:** checked mechanisms, visible refusal, corruption harness, latency report.
- **Innovation:** provenance-preserving, need-first, privacy-minimizing handoff.
- **Impact:** less hold time and fewer dead-end applications; do not quote an ACS number until the archived analysis is complete.
- **Feasibility:** public data, deterministic safety spine, and Microsoft Foundry/Azure providers with local fallbacks.

End with: “A chatbot gives an answer. Last-Mile produces a signed, recoverable action packet whose facts, sources, transformations, refusals, and handoff survive every channel. It never originates the alert or decides eligibility; it makes authoritative help easier to understand, verify, and act on.”
