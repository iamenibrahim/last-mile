Where the words come from

Every sentence about a program is quoted from a cached FEMA, eCFR, SBA or SAMHSA page, and carries a link and a character range back to it. This site writes no program facts of its own.

What it will not do

It does not decide whether you qualify, and it cannot. Only the agency running the program can do that. When the answer is unclear it says so and points you at a person.

What it keeps

Your county and the situations you ticked, for the length of the request. Not your name, address, or anything you type in the free-text box.
