Where the alert comes from

Every alert here was issued by the National Weather Service and cached. This tool restates one; it never originates one. The original text is shown beside every sentence it produces.

When it stays silent

If a rewritten sentence cannot be verified against the source, it is withheld and the original English is shown instead. An abstention is the correct answer when the alternative is a confident guess.

If the two disagree

The original wins. Act on the National Weather Service text, and call 911 if you are in danger now.
